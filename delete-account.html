Delete your InVoice account

Developer: Hamza Saeed · App: InVoice

You can delete your InVoice account and its data from this page, without installing the app. You can also do it inside the app under More → Delete account.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details
  • Your membership of any business, and your access to it
  • Your business, its invoices, line items, templates and history — if you are the last active member

What is kept, and why

If other people are still active members of your business, that business and its invoices remain, because they belong to the business rather than to you personally — deleting your account must not destroy your colleagues' records. Ownership passes to another active member, and invoices you created stay with the business under their name.

This cannot be undone. Once deleted, the account cannot be recovered and you will not be able to sign in again.
