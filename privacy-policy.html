Privacy Policy

InVoice — invoicing for small businesses

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This policy explains what InVoice collects, why, and who else sees it. It covers the Android app and the services it talks to.

What we collect

Account information

  • Email address — used to create your account, sign you in, and recover access.
  • Password — stored only as a cryptographic hash by our authentication provider. Nobody, including us, can read it.

Business and invoice data

Everything you enter in the app, which may include:

  • Your business name and details, and which team members belong to it
  • Client names, invoice numbers, dates and due dates
  • Line items, quantities, prices, taxes and totals
  • Payment records and amounts paid
  • Notes you add to an invoice
  • Invoice template settings, including any logo image and signature image you upload

Voice recordings and voice commands

When you use dictation or the voice assistant, the app records a short audio clip and sends it for processing, so your words can be turned into an invoice or a command. With a voice command the app also sends what the assistant needs to carry it out: the screen you are on and any invoice open on it; your recent invoices' numbers, client names, dates, totals, status and amounts still owed; a list of your client names; your invoice template names; your business name, address, phone and email; this phone's next invoice number; your last few voice exchanges; and, if you manage the business, the email addresses of people asking to join it.

We don't keep the audio once the request is done. Our servers log only technical details of each request (its time, your account ID, the outcome and how long it took), never the audio or what was said. Spoken replies are made by your phone's own text-to-speech.

What we do not collect. InVoice contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and no crash-reporting service. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos beyond images you explicitly pick for a template, or your device identifiers. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we do not use your data to build advertising profiles.

Who else processes your data

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts the database and handles sign-in. Your account and invoice data are stored there.
  • Cloudflare — the voice features run through Cloudflare Workers, which check that you're signed in and pass your request to Google. Cloudflare keeps short-lived technical logs of each request, not its content.
  • Google (Gemini API) — transcribes your speech and carries out voice commands. The audio and the details listed under "Voice recordings and voice commands" are sent to Google for this. We use Google's free Gemini API tier, under which Google may use what it receives to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. If you'd rather not share this, use the app without voice: everything else works the same.

Your data may be stored or processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union.

Why we collect it

  • To create and secure your account
  • To store your invoices and sync them across your devices and your team
  • To turn your speech into invoices and commands, when you choose to use voice
  • To generate the PDF documents you export and share

We do not use your data for any purpose beyond operating the app.

How long we keep it

Your account and invoice data are kept for as long as your account exists. Invoices you delete are marked deleted and removed from your devices; they may persist in backups for a limited period before being purged.

Your rights

You can:

  • Access the data held about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate — most of it is editable directly in the app
  • Request deletion of your account and its data
  • Request a copy of your data

To make any of these requests, email us at the address below. We aim to respond within 30 days.

Security

All traffic between the app and our services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access to your business's data is restricted at the database level so that only members of your business can read it.

Children

InVoice is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, notify you in the app.
